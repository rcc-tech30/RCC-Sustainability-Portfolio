Methodology and boundaries

Use this planning model as a starting point, then replace every factor and commercial assumption with evidence appropriate to your situation.

Calculation approach

Mobile-combustion Scope 1

Fuel volume is converted to energy and multiplied by the relevant Scope 1 factor. Scope 3 well-to-tank emissions are intentionally excluded.

BEV electricity

The preferred method uses transitioning vehicles, annual distance, vehicle efficiency and charging loss. The fallback converts baseline fuel energy using an illustrative efficiency factor.

Purchased-electricity Scope 2

The grid-based result uses total post-transition electricity and the selected grid factor. Certificate-adjusted Scope 2 is presented separately.

Investment and payback

Transition investment deducts resale proceeds. The alternate basis compares BEV purchase cost with a planned new ICE purchase. Payback divides investment by annual operating savings.
